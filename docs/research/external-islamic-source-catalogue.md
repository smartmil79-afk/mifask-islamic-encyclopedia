# External Islamic Research Source Catalogue

## Purpose

This catalogue records external sources that can help editors discover references for MIFASK Islamic Encyclopedia. It is a research and attribution register, not an instruction to copy or republish third-party material.

## Initial source roles

| Source | Discovery role | Editorial cautions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kalamullah | Discover titles and editions of Islamic books, lecture notes, khutbahs, and educational material. | The site could not be fully inspected during the initial review. Verify each item, author, publisher, edition, and rights holder independently. A file being publicly accessible does not establish permission to republish it. |
| Muslim Central | Discover supplementary audio lectures, speakers, and lecture series. | Record the speaker, series, episode, original page, and audio rights/terms. Do not mirror audio unless permission or a suitable licence is confirmed. |
| IslamQA | Discover questions, scholarly discussions, and cited evidences for editorial research. | Its published terms restrict reproduction/redistribution without permission and describe personal, non-commercial use. Use as a discovery/citation source unless written permission or another clear licence permits reuse. Label its stated methodology and do not imply that one site's answer represents universal scholarly consensus. |

## Editorial workflow

1. **Discovered** — source URL recorded; no content reuse implied.
2. **Metadata checked** — title, author/speaker, publisher, date, edition, and canonical URL checked where available.
3. **Rights reviewed** — licence/terms and permission status recorded for the specific item, not inferred from the website generally.
4. **Evidence extracted** — references and claims are recorded as notes in the editor's own words; quotations remain short and attributed.
5. **Scholarly review** — Qur'anic references, hadith grading/source details, translations, and differences of scholarly opinion are checked by a qualified reviewer.
6. **Approved for publication** — only original editorial writing or material with documented reuse permission/licensing is published.
7. **Published** — publication version, reviewer, date, and source snapshot are retained for audit.

## Required metadata for each discovered item

- Source and canonical URL
- Item title and item type (book, article, answer, audio, lecture series, PDF)
- Author, speaker, publisher, editor, and edition where known
- Publication date and access date
- Relevant subject tags and MIFASK article/collection mapping
- Qur'an references and hadith references as cited by the source
- Rights holder, licence URL, permission evidence, and permitted uses
- Reuse status: `unknown`, `link_only`, `metadata_only`, `permission_required`, `licensed`, or `permission_granted`
- Editorial status and reviewer notes

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- Do not scrape behind access controls, bypass rate limits, or bulk-download protected material.
- Do not copy complete articles, answers, books, transcripts, or audio into MIFASK without explicit reuse rights.
- Preserve attribution and link to the original item.
- Distinguish source claims from MIFASK editorial conclusions.
- Identify the school/methodology or stated editorial approach when relevant; record legitimate scholarly differences respectfully.
- Never invent an author, edition, hadith grading, citation, licence, or permission.
- A source link is not proof that its contents have been reviewed or verified.
- Keep imported metadata separate from publication-ready content.

## Initial research priorities

1. Prophet biographies and Qur'anic narratives
2. Seerah and early Islamic history
3. Companions and notable scholars
4. Hadith collections and explanatory works
5. Tafsir and Qur'anic studies
6. Aqeedah and fiqh, with school/methodology labels where relevant
7. Du'a and adhkar

## Acceptance criteria for the first implementation

- All three websites appear as source records with explicit reuse status.
- Editors can distinguish discovery, rights review, scholarly review, and publication.
- Every published item has an attribution trail.
- No third-party full text or audio is imported by this catalogue change.
